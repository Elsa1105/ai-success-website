Short-term liquidity

For when the business is healthy but the timing is not

Profitable companies run out of cash all the time. A customer stretches from 30 days to 75, a landlord asks for a year of rent up front, a bulk order needs paying before the season it was bought for. None of that means the business is in trouble — it means money is arriving later than it is leaving.

A working capital loan closes that gap. Funds are unsecured and unrestricted: you decide whether they go to payroll, stock, marketing or a deposit. Repayments are fixed monthly instalments, so the cost is predictable from day one.

Best suited to

  • Seasonal businessesRetail, F&B and events companies funding a build-up before peak trading.
  • Companies with slow-paying customersWhere receivables are sound but the collection cycle is long.
  • Growing headcountHiring ahead of revenue, where salaries start before the new team bills.
  • Opportunistic purchasesBulk stock at a discount, or a supplier offering better terms for early settlement.

Why SMEs choose this facility

No collateral required

The facility is unsecured. Your property, equipment and receivables stay unencumbered and available for other financing.

Funds in as little as 48 hours

Complete applications submitted before noon usually receive an indicative decision the same working day.

Fixed, predictable repayments

Equal monthly instalments across the tenure. No balloon payment, no rate resets mid-term.

Transparent all-in cost

One processing fee, disclosed up front. No early-settlement penalty after the sixth instalment.

Grows with your track record

Clients in good standing are typically offered a higher limit at renewal, often at a better rate.

A named advisor

The same person handles your application, your drawdown and your renewal. No call-centre queue.
