SME Working Capital Loan features

The Enterprise Singapore scheme our advisors most often route SME applications through.

Up to S$500K financing

Access up to S$500,000 of working capital financing under the scheme.

Up to 5 years repayment

Maximum repayment period of 5 years, with no early redemption penalty.

Government risk sharing

50% government risk sharing, rising to 70% for young companies.

New measures announced in July 2026

Enhanced government support for the SME Working Capital Loan — till March 2027

Enterprise Financing Scheme enhancements

Sep 2026 – Mar 2027

  • SME Working Capital Loan: government risk-share raised to 70%
  • Project Loan: government risk-share raised to 70%; covers domestic construction projects

One-off SME Cash Grant 2026

Nov 2026

  • S$500 per local employee for eligible SMEs
  • Capped at S$2,500 per SME

In July 2026 the government announced a new enhancement to the SME Working Capital Loan, in response to the Middle East conflict. Government risk sharing with participating financial institutions rises from 50% to 70% until March 2027.

What does this mean for SMEs? Credit conditions are likely to be more accommodative. Businesses exploring financing should look at the scheme before March 2027, while the enhanced risk-share is in place.
