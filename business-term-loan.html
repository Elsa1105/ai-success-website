Long-term investment

Fund the decision that changes the size of your business

A term loan is the right instrument when you can name the investment and estimate its payback: a second outlet, a production line, buying out a partner, or taking over a competitor’s book of clients. You take a lump sum today and repay it in equal instalments over an agreed period.

It is also the quiet workhorse of debt consolidation. Businesses that have accumulated three or four short-term facilities at different rates often cut their monthly outgoings materially by folding them into one longer term loan — without borrowing another cent.

Best suited to

  • Expansion projectsNew premises, additional outlets, or entering an adjacent market.
  • Acquisitions and buy-outsPurchasing a competitor, a client book, or a departing shareholder’s stake.
  • Major fit-outs and renovationCapital works with a multi-year useful life.
  • Debt consolidationReplacing several expensive short-term facilities with one predictable repayment.

What a term loan gives you

Substantial quantum

Facilities up to S$5 million, structured across our panel of banks and licensed financiers where a single lender cannot take the whole exposure.

Tenure that matches the payback

Up to 60 months, so repayments track the return the investment actually generates.

Fixed rate for the full term

Your instalment is set at drawdown and does not move with market rates.

Government-assisted options

Where you qualify, we route applications through Enterprise Singapore-backed schemes for sharper pricing.

Structured drawdown

For staged projects, funds can be released in tranches so you are not paying interest on idle cash.

One point of accountability

We manage the lender relationship, the documentation and the conditions precedent on your behalf.
